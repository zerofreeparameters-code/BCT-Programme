# Gate DYN — Pre-Registration

**Question.** Between 1 January 2024 and the run date, has any published or
preprinted work exhibited *induced gravitational dynamics*, not merely an
effective metric, in an analogue-gravity or emergent-gravity system? If so,
what microscopic ingredients does it need, and is any such construction
available to a vacuum whose only field is a single-component compact scalar
order parameter (one complex scalar, vacuum manifold S¹, global U(1), no other
fields)?

**Type.** ASSESSMENT gate: literature census plus classification. Not a
construction gate. No new model, Lagrangian, induced-gravity calculation or
rescue route may be written in this session. Writing one voids the run.

**Claim under test.** The operator's ledger records as settled prior art:
"analogue models reproduce GR kinematics, not dynamics." This gate checks that
statement against the literature since 2024. A null result corroborates it. A
positive result reopens it.

**Pre-registered.** 10 October 2026, as Gate 2 of the order set on

## §2 Definitions (fixed before searching)

**Kinematic analogue (L0).** A field theory whose excitations propagate as if
on a Lorentzian effective metric g_eff set by a background (flow, condensate
density, medium). The background obeys its own equations (continuity, Euler,
Gross–Pitaevskii and so on), not Einstein's.

**Dynamics ladder.** Place each candidate at the highest level whose condition
it *exhibits*.

- **L1 Backreaction.** The excitations' stress-energy modifies the background,
  and this is computed, but the equation governing g_eff is not of Einstein
  form.
- **L2 Newtonian or scalar gravity.** An emergent long-range interaction obeys
  a Poisson-type equation (∇²Φ = 4πG_eff ρ, a Λ-like term allowed), or a
  relativistic scalar (Nordström-type) field equation, sourced universally by
  the quasiparticles' energy.
- **L3 Linearised tensor gravity.** A massless spin-2 mode with two propagating
  polarisations, coupled universally to the conserved stress-energy of all
  low-energy excitations, obeying the linearised Einstein (Fierz–Pauli)
  equations at long wavelength.
- **L4 Full Einstein dynamics.** An effective action for g_eff that is
  diffeomorphism-invariant and reduces at long wavelength to the
  Einstein–Hilbert form √−g R / (16πG_eff), Λ allowed, derived rather than
  assumed, with universal matter coupling.

**Tags.** Record every tag that applies.

- FERMIONS: needs fermionic constituents or quasiparticles.
- MULTI: needs more than one bosonic field or condensate component.
- TENSOR: needs a vector, spinor or tensor order parameter (for example the
  orbital and spin structure of superfluid helium-3).
- GAUGE: needs an added gauge connection.
- SPECIES: needs many fields or species (Sakharov-type induction with N ≫ 1).
- FINE-TUNE: needs tuned parameters or constraints to suppress
  non-Einsteinian terms.
- LV: relies on fundamental Lorentz violation (for example as the escape from
  the Weinberg–Witten theorem).
- ASSUMED: the Einstein form is put in rather than derived.
- NUMERICAL, EXPERIMENTAL: the evidence is simulation or laboratory data.

**Single-scalar availability.** A candidate is *single-scalar-available* if and
only if it reaches its level with exactly one complex scalar order parameter,
vacuum manifold S¹, global U(1), and no added fields or connections, that is,
with none of FERMIONS, MULTI, TENSOR, GAUGE or SPECIES. FINE-TUNE, LV,
NUMERICAL and EXPERIMENTAL do not disqualify on their own; record them anyway.
A candidate tagged ASSUMED is placed at L1 at most, whatever it claims.

recent Barceló–Liberati–Visser review you can reach, verifying its

external statement of record.

**Candidate step.** For every in-window work that claims any level of L1 or
above, read the full text as far as the derivation and record: citation, date,
venue, system, level reached with equation or page reference, tags, and
single-scalar availability. If it is paywalled with no preprint, record it as
UNVERIFIED with its claimed level.

## §5 Stop conditions

Evaluate in this order. The first that fires is the verdict.

- **SC-DYN-4 (indeterminate).** The census could not reach at least two of
  arXiv, INSPIRE-HEP and the review in the baseline step; or any UNVERIFIED or
  UNFETCHED candidate claims L3 or L4 and would be single-scalar-available.
  Halt and name exactly what the operator must retrieve.
- **SC-DYN-3 (reopen).** At least one verified in-window candidate reaches L3
  or L4 and is single-scalar-available. Halt once it is fully recorded. Do not
  assess what it means for any particular model.
- **SC-DYN-2 (refine).** At least one verified in-window candidate reaches L2,
  or reaches L3 or L4 only with a disqualifying tag. The kinematics-only
  statement is refined to "dynamics at level Lk requires {tags}", listing each
  level reached and what it needed. An L2 result does not reach tensor gravity
  and is recorded as such.
- **SC-DYN-1 (corroborate).** No verified in-window candidate exceeds L1. The
  kinematics-only statement is externally corroborated as of the run date.
