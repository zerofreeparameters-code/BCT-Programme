PART A — THE DECOUPLING THEOREM (the load-bearing step of this gate)
==============================================================================
S_gauge = beta (1 - cos(k a))            ->  quadratic: A**2*beta*k**2/2
S_med (neutral)                          = J*k**2*s**2/2 + J*s**4/4
  d S_med / d a  = 0    <-- zero: no a-dependence at all
  mixed Hessian block  d^2 S / da ds  = 0

Integrating the medium out exactly (Gaussian case):
  Z_med = Int ds exp(-S_med) = sqrt(2)*sqrt(pi)/(sqrt(J)*k)
  d Z_med / d a = 0
  induced effective action for a:  Gamma_eff = -ln Z_med = log(J)/2 + log(k) - log(pi)/2 - log(2)/2
  d Gamma_eff / d a = 0   -> the medium induces NO a-dependent term

  => beta_effective = beta_bare EXACTLY. For the pinned action the medium cannot
     renormalise beta, let alone fix it. beta is an input of S_gauge and nothing else.
     This closes R2, R3 and R5-without-added-charged-matter in one step:
       R2: an induced Maxwell term requires a Psi-a coupling; there is none.
       R3: beta and J are independent coefficients of two decoupled terms.
       R5: a matter loop requires matter charged under a; the medium is not.

--- Does the Gauss-law constraint evade the factorisation? ---
The card §1 imposes Gauss's law at the nodes. That constraint reads div E = rho, where rho
is the charge density of whatever is charged under a_e. With the medium NEUTRAL (option B)
the medium contributes rho = 0 identically, so the constraint is a condition on the gauge
sector alone and the factorisation is untouched. The constraint becomes a coupling only
when something carries charge - which is Gate DL-MATTER's separate input, and which for the
medium itself is R1 (Meissner mass, Part B). So the one structural element of the
construction that touches the nodes does not connect the medium to beta either.

PART B — R1 KILL TEST: minimal coupling gives the Meissner mass
==============================================================================
CHARGED medium  |(d - i q a)Psi|^2  ->  L = A**2*phi0**2*q**2 - 2*A*dtheta*phi0**2*q + dtheta**2*phi0**2
  photon mass^2 (unitary gauge) = (1/2) d^2L/da^2 = phi0**2*q**2
  = q^2 rho_s, NONZERO for q != 0  ->  Meissner/London mass (Gate LINK's fate).
  A charged medium is not the neutral medium of option B: R1 CONTRADICTS the
  construction it is meant to serve. Route closed by the card's own kill test.

  Note the structural tension this exposes, which is the finding of the gate:
  q != 0 is the ONLY way to make the medium's action depend on a without adding a
  new coupling, and it is exactly the thing that gaps the photon. Neutrality buys
  masslessness (CN-DL-02) and pays for it with beta.
