## 1 — STEP 0: WHAT IS THE CORPUS'S VARIABLE, ACTUALLY?

From primary source, and quoting the text:

- Write out every dynamical phase variable the corpus defines, with its index structure. State for
  each whether the index runs over **sites**, **links**, or neither.
- For each, establish whether it is canonical: does it have a kinetic term and a conjugate
  momentum, or is it algebraically fixed by something else?
- Recount the propagating degrees of freedom from the Hamiltonian as written. Show the count.
- State whether the corpus **anywhere** commits to a reading, explicitly or by the structure of an
  equation. A Hamiltonian summed over ⟨i,j⟩ of cos(θ_i − θ_j) is site-valued; a Hamiltonian over
  plaquettes of link phases is link-valued. Which does the corpus write?

## 2 — STEP 1: WHAT WOULD THE LINK THEORY REQUIRE?

Construct, explicitly:

- The link-variable Hamiltonian for compact U(1) on the BCT lattice, with its plaquette term.
- The Gauss-law constraint at each site, and what it constrains.
- The degree-of-freedom count for that theory, derived rather than asserted.
- The continuum limit, and whether it is Maxwell.

**Then state what must be added to BCT to get there**, itemised: new variables, new constraints,
new terms, and anything that must be **removed or reinterpreted**. This is the price tag and it is
the point of the gate.

## 3 — STEP 2: IS IT AVAILABLE, OR DOES SOMETHING BLOCK IT?

Test each of these against the corpus, and report what you find rather than what fits:

- **The phonon identification.** Does the corpus identify the photon with a phonon or shear wave
  of the medium? If so, does that identification survive a link reformulation, or is it lost?
- **The vortex/charge identification.** Matter is vortices of the phase. In a link theory, what
  are the charges, and does the identification survive?
- **α₀ and the transfer matrix.** App D.3 derives the coupling from void transmission amplitudes.
  Does that derivation presuppose a site reading? Would it survive?
- **Confinement.** Compact U(1) in 3+1D lattice gauge theory has a confining phase at strong
  coupling. Where would BCT sit, and does the corpus's own language about a Coulomb phase match?
- **The healing length.** Whether ξ relative to the lattice spacing bears on which formulation is
  physically appropriate. Compute the ratio from source.

## 4 — STEP 3: THE OBSTRUCTION CHECK

Independently of the reformulation, establish whether anything in the corpus **forbids** 2
polarisations on general grounds — a stated rank or helicity argument, a symmetry obstruction, a
representation-theoretic result. Quote it if it exists. **If such an argument exists and is sound,
the reformulation is moot and the gate closes there.**
