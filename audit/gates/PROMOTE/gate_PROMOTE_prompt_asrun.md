## HYPOTHETICAL-CONSTRUCTION gate — audits whether a promotion M = S¹ → (larger manifold) is *geometry-selected* or a free input; does NOT repair or vindicate the written corpus (see §0.5).

**The question.**

> The written theory is a single **S¹** complex scalar (one helicity-0 Goldstone). Three of the
> programme's target physics fail for one shared reason: a photon needs a **transverse vector** (2
> polarisations), dynamical gravity needs a **tensor** mode, and the round periodic table needs
> **π₃(S²) ≠ 0** — none of which a single S¹ scalar carries. The generic escape ("promote the vacuum
> manifold M beyond S¹") is **free**: anyone may declare M = S² or a tensor manifold because it would
> be convenient. A free promotion is a new parameter, not a derivation. The only gate-worthy version
> is **geometry-*selected***: the BCT geometry itself must force or prefer a specific
> symmetry-breaking pattern G → H with dim(M) > 1, with no fit.
>
> **So: is there a principled, geometry-selected reason for the BCT vacuum manifold to be larger than
> S¹ — and if so, TO WHAT — and does anything of the α layer (α₀ = r_oct·r_tet/π) survive the
> promotion?** Two selection bars, both to be reported: **FORCE** — the BCT geometry's energetics
> select a G → H ground state with dim(M) > 1 over the S¹ scalar; **PREFER** — a *stated* geometric
> principle (the void network, the O_h point group's irrep content, the D4/octonion structure) picks
> a specific multi-dimensional order-parameter irrep, with no selected fit. The **decisive physics** is
> the isotropy pincer: BCT's best-surviving result is exact acoustic isotropy at c/a = √2, and
> *condensing* an anisotropy-inducing multi-dimensional irrep breaks it, while 1-dimensional irreps
> give only a **discrete** manifold (no Goldstones, no photon). The single continuous route that does
> not break isotropy is **richer *local* degrees of freedom (a vector/tensor on sites or genuine link
> variables) sitting in an O_h-symmetric, quantum-*disordered* ground state with emergent gauge
> modes** — and that route lives or dies on one thing: **can richer local DOF supply the HARD local
> Gauss-law constraint that a neutral single scalar provably cannot** (a neutral scalar's double layer
> is a soft coupling with no separable charge)? If yes, and it deconfines on this lattice as a true
> transverse vector, the promotion is geometry-hosted and the CP¹ photon becomes reachable; if no, the
> promotion reduces to the already-exhausted link/window routes and buys nothing.

## 8 — STOP CONDITIONS

**SC-PROMOTE-1.** The geometry selects **NO** promotion under either bar (neither forces nor prefers a
multi-dimensional irrep without a fit). → **S¹ stands;** the manifold promotion is a free input;
route closed.
**SC-PROMOTE-2.** The geometry selects only irreps whose **condensation breaks the isotropy bound.**

**SC-PROMOTE-3.** The geometry selects only **1-dimensional** irreps. → M is **discrete**, no
Goldstones, no continuous manifold, no photon.
**SC-PROMOTE-4.** A bar is met (state **FORCE or PREFER**) **AND** richer local DOF supply an
**isotropy-safe hard Gauss-law constraint** that **deconfines** on this lattice with a **true
transverse vector** (guarded against scalar-phonon and Kalb–Ramond false positives). →
**Favourable: the first geometry-hosted route to a massless mode beyond the phonon.** Hand
target-selection (Step 3) and α-survival (Step 4) to the conditional sub-steps; record the Step-4

**SC-PROMOTE-5.** A bar may be met, but richer local DOF **still supply no hard constraint** (the
neutral-scalar failure persists for non-scalar DOF: soft, or no separable charge, or the lattice
confines). → The promotion reduces to the already-exhausted link/window routes and **buys no photon.**
**SC-PROMOTE-6.** The reduction is **inapplicable** / the promoted theory is **unauditable** as posed
(the candidate action cannot be pinned down). → **Halt / report as ill-posed.**
