Part A: is there an energy window where charges and the emergent photon are both light?
==============================================================================
  Granting the Coulomb phase of VOID-Q2: photon band top ~ pi * J_ring, J_ring ~ t^L / U^(L-1);
  charges (rule violations) cost the Mott gap Delta ~ U (it closes only at the Mott edge).
  Running of speeds needs energies E with Delta < E < pi J_ring. Ratio Delta/(pi J_ring) = (U/t)^L / pi.

  Reading: the photon's whole band lies below the charge gap by (U/t)^L. Charges never run
  as light particles while the photon exists, so nothing pulls the speeds together. The
  photon's stiffness J_ring is itself the virtual-charge effect; it is already counted.
  A window opens only within (pi J/U)^2 e^(-2l) of the Mott edge, where the charges are about to
  condense; condensed charges Higgs the photon (it gets a mass: the TWIST outcome again).

Part C: final approach (speeds nearly equal) and the observational bounds
==============================================================================
  Near equal speeds: d delta/dl = -(2 alpha/pi) delta and d alpha/dl = -(2/3pi) alpha^2
  give delta_IR/delta_UV = (alpha_IR/alpha_UV)^3. alpha_IR is measured (1/137); weak coupling
  needs alpha_UV <~ 1, and one-loop QED reaches alpha_UV = 1 only after (3pi/2)(137 - 1) e-folds.
    e-folds from alpha = 1/137 up to alpha = 1: 641 (available 51.5)
    best possible reduction at weak coupling: (1/137)^3 = 3.89e-07
    over the real 51.5 e-folds: alpha_UV = 1/126.1, reduction (alpha_IR/alpha_UV)^3 = 0.779
    numeric check: delta 1e-2 -> 7.7916e-03 over 51.5 e-folds ((alpha ratio)^3 predicts 7.7916e-03)

  Observed limits on the electron-photon speed difference:
    LEP/Tevatron (no vacuum Cherenkov, stable photons): -5.8e-12 <= k_tr - (4/3) c_e00 <= 1.2e-11
    astrophysical (Altschul, as quoted by Anber & Donoghue): |eta| <~ 1e-14
    delta_UV = 1 (after the fast phase): weak-coupling floor 3.9e-07;  51.5 e-folds give 7.8e-01
    delta_UV = 1e-2 (Collins et al. size): weak-coupling floor 3.9e-09;  51.5 e-folds give 7.8e-03
  To reach 1e-14 at weak coupling the starting mismatch would already have to be <= 2.6e-08.

==============================================================================
Summary
==============================================================================
  A. In the void Coulomb phase there is no window: the charge gap sits (U/t)^L above the photon's
     whole band. Nothing runs. Opening a window means tuning to the Mott edge, where the photon
     is Higgsed.
  B. Granting light charges, one loop drags the photon up only as 1/sqrt(l): R0 = 2.8e4 ends at
     ~600 (alpha0 = 1/137) to ~50 (alpha0 = 1) after the largest window physics offers, and the
     same fast charges screen the coupling: alpha_IR ends 4x to 50x below 1/137.
  C. The last stretch is logarithmic (delta ~ alpha^3). Best case at weak coupling: a factor 4e-7,
     and that needs 641 e-folds (12x more than exist): short of 1e-11 (lab) / 1e-14 (astro) by
     4.5 / 7.4 orders. Over the real 51.5 e-folds the factor is 0.78: short by 11 / 14 orders.
