\textbf{Abstract (as written in the draft).}
Appendix~J conjectured that a 21-vertex fermionic loop might generate
$\me\approx\az^{21/2}$. This appendix constructs the BCT lattice Green's
function and derives three theorems. \textbf{T1 (Bipartite):} the lattice is

$a_{16}\approx\me/4$ proximity is a numerical accident. \emph{See the audit
notice: T1 and T2 assume coordination 8; the correct $c/a=\sqrt2$ lattice is
FCC (coordination 12, non-bipartite), on which T1 and T2 fail.}

\section{The BCT Lattice Hamiltonian (as written)}
Two sublattices: A (corners) at $(n_1,n_2,n_3\sqrt2)$, B (body centres) at
$(n_1+\tfrac12,n_2+\tfrac12,(n_3+\tfrac12)\sqrt2)$. The draft takes each A site to have 8 NN B
sites via $\delta_{\pm\pm\pm}=(\pm\tfrac12,\pm\tfrac12,\pm\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2})$, each of length
$1\,\ell_P$. \emph{[Audit: the 4 in-plane A--A bonds at distance 1 are omitted; see banner.]}
The fermion hops with amplitude $t=\az$ (the App~J Josephson coupling):
\begin{align}
  H(k) &= t\,\gamma(k)\,\sigma_+ + t\,\gamma^*(k)\,\sigma_-,\\
  \gamma(k) &= \textstyle\sum_\delta e^{ik\cdot\delta}
    = 8\cos(k_x/2)\cos(k_y/2)\cos(k_z/\sqrt2),\\
  E_\pm(k) &= \pm t\,|\gamma(k)| = \pm 8\az\,|\cos(k_x/2)\cos(k_y/2)\cos(k_z/\sqrt2)|.
\end{align}
Bandwidth $16\az=0.11853$ (Planck units); spectrum symmetric about $E=0$ \emph{in this
(8-coordination) model}.

\section{The Green's Function (as written)}
\begin{align}
  G_{AA}(k,\omega)=G_{BB}(k,\omega) &= \frac{\omega}{\omega^2-\az^2|\gamma(k)|^2},\\
  G_{AB}(k,\omega) &= \frac{\az\gamma(k)}{\omega^2-\az^2|\gamma(k)|^2}.
\end{align}
The structure factor vanishes on three nodal planes $k_x=\pi$, $k_y=\pi$, $k_z=\pi/\sqrt2$; near
them $E\approx v_\perp|k_\perp|$ with $v_\perp=4\az$, giving $\rho(E)\sim E\ln(1/E)$ (a 3D
graphene-like linear-with-log DOS). The draft concludes, by the assumed PH symmetry,
\begin{equation}
  \Gloc(0)=2\omega\!\int_0^\Lambda\!\frac{\rho(E)\,dE}{\omega^2-E^2}\Big|_{\omega=0}=0,\qquad
  G_{AB,\mathrm{loc}}(0)=0.
\end{equation}
\emph{[Audit: on the correct FCC band $[-4t,+12t]$ the DOS is PH-asymmetric and $\Gloc(0)\neq0$.]}

\section{Theorem 2 --- Non-Renormalization of the Zero Mode (as written)}
\textbf{Claim.} $\Sigma^{(n)}(\omega{=}0)=0$ for all $n\ge1$: the zero-energy fermion acquires zero
mass from the sphere--void coupling at all orders, because each self-energy term carries a factor
$\Gloc(0)=0$. The draft reads this as protection identical to graphene/Weyl nodes, and adds the
corollary that the photon and graviton (also zero modes) are \emph{exactly} massless.
\emph{[Audit: the protection relied on PH symmetry, which fails on the FCC band; $\Gloc(0)\neq0$, so
the zero mode is unprotected and the electron gets a (too-large) perturbative mass. The
exactly-massless photon/graviton corollary loses its proof.]}

\section{Theorem 3 --- The Schwinger--Dyson Gap Equation (as written)}
In the local approximation the gap equation $1=t^2\!\int dE\,\rho(E)/(E^2+\me^2)$ with the linear
DOS gives $\me=\Lambda\exp(-\sqrt{2/(ct^2)})$, $\Lambda=8t$. The draft finds $c\,t^2\approx0.02$ is
required but BCT provides $\approx0.001$ --- a factor $\sim20$ too weak for dynamical mass
generation. \emph{[Audit: the DOS input is from the 8-coordination model; recompute on the FCC
band --- Gate KAUDIT.]}

\section{The $n=16$ Near-Coincidence --- Explained as an Accident (as written)}
$a_{16}=\az^{16}C_A(16)/16=1.0962\times10^{-26}\approx\me/4$. The draft correctly attributes this
to the monotone series $a_{2m}\approx(8\az^2)(1/15)^{m-1}$ inevitably passing through the scale of
$\me/\mP=4.2\times10^{-23}$ near $n\approx16$, with \emph{no physical content} --- and notes T2
sums the series to zero anyway. \emph{[Audit: this anti-numerology conclusion is correct and
commendable.]}
