**Description (paste verbatim into Zenodo abstract field):**

We present a complete geometric analysis of the BCT Superfluid Lattice vacuum, formalising the physical grid of the quantum vacuum as an infinite D4 root lattice of Planck-scale packed spheres. Starting from three geometric inputs alone — the octahedral void radius r_oct = (√2−1)/2, the tetrahedral void radius r_tet = (√6−2)/4, and the BCT density cap ρ_max = 6.302 — we derive (i) the exact packing fraction η = π/(3√2) ≈ 0.7405 and the relative volumes of the two void species; (ii) the physical sphere radius in Planck units, R_sphere ≈ 0.2432 ℓ_P; (iii) a quantised tunnelling formalism showing how energy propagates through the void network as Josephson channels encoding U(1) × SU(2) × SU(3); and (iv) a demonstration that the D4 kissing number K = 24 constitutes a hard ceiling on local degrees of freedom, fully accounting for the observed particle content of the Standard Model. All results follow from geometry with zero free parameters.
