## 1. THE ACTUAL PROBLEM

Global hunger is not caused by insufficient food production.
It is caused by three things:

**1.1 Fertiliser cost and access**
Nitrogen fertiliser is the single largest input cost for
subsistence farmers in the developing world. It is derived
from the Haber-Bosch process — energy intensive, expensive,
controlled by a small number of corporations, and completely
inaccessible to smallholder farmers without capital.

If you cannot afford fertiliser, your yield drops by 50-70%.
If your yield drops by 50-70%, you cannot feed your family.
If you cannot feed your family, your children do not develop
properly. If your children do not develop properly, the next
generation cannot escape poverty.

This is the nitrogen trap. 800 million people are in it.

**BCT-N2FIX** breaks the nitrogen trap.

**1.2 Water access and quality**
1.1 billion people lack access to clean water.
Contaminated water causes crop failure, livestock death,
and direct human mortality. Current filtration technology
requires energy, infrastructure, and capital that subsistence
communities do not have.

**BCT-WATER** addresses this.

**1.3 Distribution — the geometry problem**
Food exists. It does not reach hungry people because the
distribution system has broken symmetry. Topological charge
(food, money, resources) accumulates at nodes (wealthy
countries, corporations, cities) and cannot flow to voids
(rural subsistence communities, conflict zones, climate-
affected regions).

**BCT-EQUITY** (Patent #27) addresses the distribution geometry.
