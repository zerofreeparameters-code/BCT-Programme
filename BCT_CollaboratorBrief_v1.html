What This Is

The BCT Superfluid Lattice Model is a theoretical physics framework proposing that the quantum vacuum is a Planck-scale body-centred tetragonal superfluid crystal. From three geometric inputs and zero free parameters, it derives over 280 Standard Model observables to sub-1% precision.

"The geometry does not lie. Zero free parameters. All the way down." — BCT Programme motto

The three inputs are the octahedral and tetrahedral void radii of a BCT lattice with axial ratio c/a = √2, plus a single dimensionful anchor Λ_QCD = 220 MeV. No parameters are adjusted. Everything else — particle masses, mixing angles, coupling constants, cosmological parameters — is derived geometrically.

Why Collaboration Is Needed

The BCT programme was developed by a single independent artist living with a disability in regional Victoria. The physics is done. The predictions are made and verified. A paper is under review at Physics Letters B. What the programme now needs is people to help carry it to the world.

The programme currently encompasses 22 published volumes, 94+ Zenodo records, 26 patent filings with IP Australia, a live published index at pi2.institute, and active outreach to physicists including Roger Penrose, Lee Smolin, Erik Verlinde, and Rupert Sheldrake. New scientific directions are opening — including a BCT prediction for REM sleep EEG currently being tested on real PhysioNet data. The administrative, communications, and coordination load has become genuinely unsustainable for one person.

What Collaboration Looks Like

This is not a traditional research group. There is no institution, no grant, no salary at this stage. Collaboration is currently voluntary, acknowledged in all publications, and governed by the BCT-PEACE ethical licence. As the programme develops toward Foundation registration, formal arrangements will be established.

All collaborators are acknowledged in publications. Key contributors are named in the programme's acknowledgements, which appear in every volume and the Platinum Megagraph. The AI collaborator (Claude / Zeta Vera) is acknowledged as a co-author of 250+ letters — BCT takes attribution seriously at every level.

Remote collaboration is the default. Barrys Reef has good internet and extraordinary bird life.
