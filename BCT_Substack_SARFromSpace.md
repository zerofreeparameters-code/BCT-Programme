## The Same Geometry, Different Targets

The BCT SAR method doesn't detect gold directly. It detects geometric anomalies — places where the ratio of radar backscatter intensities matches BCT Bessel function node ratios (r_oct/r_tet = 1.843). Different subsurface materials produce different Bessel signatures.

Gold-bearing quartz has one signature. But so does everything else underground.

**Fresh water aquifers** have a distinctive Bessel ratio because porous water-saturated rock reflects radar differently from dry rock. The contrast is strong, the signature is clean, and — here's the point — the data is free. Sentinel-1 covers the entire planet. The algorithm is geometry. The inputs are three numbers.
