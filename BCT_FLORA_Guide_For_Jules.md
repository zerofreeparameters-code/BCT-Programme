## 1. TONIGHT — Glowing Flowers (30 minutes)

### UV Blue Glow
- Buy white roses, carnations, or chrysanthemums
- Stand them in a vase of **tonic water** (yes, Schweppes)
- Wait 24 hours — the quinine travels up the xylem into the petals
- Shine a **UV blacklight strip** ($10 from Bunnings or eBay)
- Watch them glow **bright electric blue**
- Cost: $5 tonic water + $10 UV strip = $15

### Green Glow in the Dark
- Mix **strontium aluminate glow powder** ($10 eBay) with water
- Stand white flowers in the solution overnight
- They'll absorb the phosphorescent particles through the stem
- Charge in sunlight during the day
- They glow **green** in the dark — no UV needed
- The glow fades over a few hours, recharges in light

### Combo Method (the full BCT)

## 2. THIS WEEK — Blue Flowers

### Instant Blue (food colouring method)
- White flowers + blue food colouring in water
- 24-48 hours for full colour uptake
- Split stems for two-tone effects (half in blue, half in red)
- You already know this trick — it's florist 101

### Natural Blue (hydrangeas)
- **Acidic soil** (pH 5.2-5.5) activates aluminium uptake
- Add **aluminium sulphate** to the soil (garden centre, $8)
- The aluminium complexes with delphinidin pigment → BLUE
- Same hydrangea, different soil = different colour
- The chemistry is: Al³⁺ ions + delphinidin = blue complex
- Works on existing hydrangea plants in 1 season

## 4. TRANSLUCENT FLOWERS — The Skeleton Flower

### Diphylleia grayi (Skeleton Flower)
- Native to Japan and China
- White petals turn **completely transparent** when wet
- The cells fill with water, matching the refractive index
- When they dry, they turn white again
- Like magic. Every single time.

### Growing Skeleton Flowers
- They like **shade and moisture** — perfect for Melbourne climate
- Rich, humus-heavy, acidic soil (pH 5.5-6.0)
- Plant in a sheltered spot — under trees is ideal
- They're woodland plants, treat them like hellebores
- Hardy to about -15°C — no problem in Victoria
- Seeds available from specialist suppliers or eBay
- Germination is SLOW (can take months) — be patient
- Once established they spread by rhizome
- Mist them with a spray bottle → instant transparency → customers gasp

### For the Shop
